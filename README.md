**Mathematics · Computational imaging · Numerical methods · Machine learning**

I’m Keneth Mayombwe, a master's student in the Erasmus Mundus Interdisciplinary Mathematics (*InterMaths*) programme, currently specialising in **computational imaging** at Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH), Germany.

My interests lie at the intersection of mathematics, computation and imaging technology. I am particularly interested in understanding how mathematical models and numerical algorithms can be used to solve practical problems in image formation, reconstruction and analysis.

## Areas of interest

- **Computational and medical imaging** — image formation, reconstruction and quantitative image analysis
- **Inverse problems** — regularisation, optimisation and iterative reconstruction methods
- **Signal processing and computer vision** — Fourier methods, filtering and image analysis
- **Numerical mathematics** — linear algebra, numerical optimisation and mathematical modelling
- **Machine learning** — predictive modelling and data-driven methods, including applications to imaging

## Technical toolkit

- **Programming:** Python, Julia, and C (basic)
- **Scientific computing:** NumPy, pandas and scikit-learn; coursework covering TensorFlow/Keras and PyTorch
- **Tools:** Git, GitHub, Linux, Jupyter and LaTeX

## Professional background

Alongside my academic work, I have experience in university-level mathematics teaching and educational publishing, including manuscript editing, educational content development and quality assurance.
